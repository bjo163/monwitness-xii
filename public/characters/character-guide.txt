CANONICAL ART DIRECTION
Characters are human in silhouette and faceless by design. Their entire faces
stay hidden behind shadow, hoods, or featureless visors. Identify each character
through outfit, props, accent color, posture, and comic sound effects. Keep the
premium ink-and-risograph graphic-novel treatment coherent across the four.
Canonical character cards use WebP for page weight; original prompt-generated
PNGs remain in the Codex generated-images archive. Vector explorations live in
styles/vector/. Add variants without overwriting canonical art. Name variants
character--pose--style.webp (example: four-lah--wave--risograph.webp).
