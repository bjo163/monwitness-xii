CANONICAL ART DIRECTION
Characters are human in silhouette and faceless by design. Their entire faces
stay hidden behind shadow, hoods, or featureless visors. Identify each character
through outfit, props, accent color, posture, and comic sound effects. Keep the
premium ink-and-risograph graphic-novel treatment coherent across the four.
The current team page and Archive spotlight use WebP variants in
styles/faceless-visor/. Their entire face areas are flat, opaque black with no
eyes, slits, reflections, skin, or facial marks. The RockSoul variant follows
the character brief: a backwards green cap, hoodie, skateboard, and dog.
Original crew images remain unchanged as visual history. The hero art keeps its
rear-facing silhouette. Vector explorations live in styles/vector/; they are
not canonical character portraits. Add new treatments without overwriting art;
name variants character--pose--style.webp (example:
four-lah--wave--risograph.webp).
