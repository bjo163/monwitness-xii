## Handling rules

- Keep original manuscripts, source copies, witness details, permissions, and other private evidence in an owner-approved private store. This repository may be public.
- Put only a stable reference to the private evidence in this register. Do not paste manuscript text, personal data, credentials, or permission documents here.
- Do not infer permission from possession, publication elsewhere, a public index, or an unreviewed URL.
- Leave each entry `source_needed` until its source identity, chain of custody, checksum, credits, reuse terms, restrictions, and privacy implications have been checked by an authorized reviewer.
- Record who reviewed the evidence, when, and the decision. Unclear, conflicting, missing, or expired permission stays `source_needed`.
- Updating this register does not change application or database status. Any status change requires an approved per-entry decision and a separately reviewed implementation change.
